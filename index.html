Methodology, Standards & Ethics

Transparent documentation of data processing algorithms, reference guidelines, unverified item exclusions, and non-judgmental guidance principles.

Purpose & Tone Statement

This dashboard visualizes how everyday patterns in student eating, drinking, sleep, and physical activity relate to subjective energy and mood. The tone of this dashboard is intentionally encouraging, supportive, and non-judgmental. We never describe an individual student or habit as "bad" or "unhealthy." Every result is framed as an observational pattern in this specific sample, not clinical advice or direct medical causality.

Dietary Reference Limits & Citations

  • Free Sugar: Under 5% of total caloric energy, equivalent to about 25 g per day for a standard 2,000 kcal diet (ICMR-NIN Dietary Guidelines for Indians, 2024).
  • Caffeine: Approximately 300 mg per day for healthy adults, as commonly cited in Indian nutritional and dietary guidance.
  • Salt / Sodium: Under 5 g of salt per day (approximately 2,000 mg of sodium) as recommended by ICMR-NIN and WHO.
  • Fruits & Vegetables: At least 400 g per day of fresh, minimally processed produce (ICMR-NIN 2024).
Excluded Unverified Records Count:

A total of 138 product consumption events involving unverified SKUs were automatically excluded from cumulative nutrient sums to prevent unconfirmed estimates from distorting statistical findings.
